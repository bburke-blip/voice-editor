    Notes

      Table read

      Each character reads their own lines in their own voice. Names are not read out. The main Voice at the top is the narrator (stage manager): it reads scene headings, action and transitions, and no character is given its voice.

        What to say

        • pause or stop, hold on, wait. Space bar works too.
        • make a note ... attaches to the section you're on, e.g. "make a note on this section to rewrite the ending". Works while it's reading.
        • make a note then say it at your own pace; "done" to finish
        • continue or keep going, carry on
        • go back · next a section at a time
        • go to [words] the section with those words · go to the top
        • repeat reads the section again
        • read the note · delete the note · undo · save

        Click any section to read from there. Turn the mic off to type straight into the script.

        PDFs open too; edits save as a Word file. Say "comma", "period" or "question mark" to add punctuation. "Dash" becomes two hyphens. Turn the mic off to type, select, copy and paste straight in the script. Headphones stop the mic hearing the reading voice.

        Pronunciation

        How the voice says things. The document itself is not changed. Screenplay terms are built in; add names and words it gets wrong. You can also say "pronounce Siobhan as shiv-awn".
